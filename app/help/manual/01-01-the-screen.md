# What MCP Server is, and how the screen is laid out

MCP Server hosts the **tools** and **skills** that the domain's applications and agents share. A tool is a function
the service runs for a caller (a web search, a URL fetch, a sensor of the SDLC assessment); a skill is an
instruction several callers must follow the same way (a citation convention, a language rule). Each is **offered to
apps**: an application or an agent connects as one app, with its **key**, and sees what that app is offered.

The **MCP Console** is where you administer all of it. Nothing here runs a model: the console shows what is
offered, lets you change it, and keeps the record of every call.

## The screen

- **Top menu** — *Catalog, Apps, Activity, View, Monitoring, Help*. At the right, your name and photo: its menu
  switches the theme and the language, and signs you out.
- **Side menu** (left) — the views: *Overview*, under **Workspace**; *Tools*, *Skills*, *Apps*, under **Catalog**;
  *Activity*, under **Operate**. *Settings* is at the bottom: its menu holds the theme, the language and the Settings view. The round
  button on the menu's edge collapses it to icons; on a tablet it starts collapsed, and on a phone it is the bottom bar.
- **Middle area** — the view you chose. A tool or a skill opens in a **drawer** at the right with its own tabs:
  *Overview, Schema, Try it, Connect, Activity, History, Edit*.
- **Status bar** (bottom) — from the left: the service, the view you are in and its details; at the right: the
  backends, the keys mode, the record, and the connection.

## Steps to get oriented

1. Look at the **Overview**: four tiles (tools, skills, apps, calls in the last 24 hours), the list of what **needs
   attention**, and the last calls.
2. Click **Tools** in the side menu and select a row: the drawer opens with everything about that tool.
3. Open **Help** › **User Manual** at any time to come back here. Its contents are in the column at the left:
   click a page to open it.
