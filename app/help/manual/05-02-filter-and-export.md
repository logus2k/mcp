# Filter and export the activity

## Steps

1. Choose the **window**: 1 h, 24 h, 7 d, 30 d (the chips, or **Activity** in the top menu).
2. Choose an **app**, a **status** (ok or error), or type in the search field: it matches the tool, the caller, the
   person and the error text.
3. **Export CSV** downloads the current filter.

## Notes

- Rows are kept for the retention period (90 days by default), then pruned.
- Settings offers a 30-day CSV in one click.
