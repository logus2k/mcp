# Revoke or rotate a key

## Revoke

1. In the app's card, click **Revoke** beside the key, then **Confirm revoke**.
2. Programs using it are refused at once (*the key presented is unknown, revoked or expired*).

## Rotate

1. Issue a new key to the app.
2. Switch the program to the new key.
3. Revoke the old one.

## Notes

- A key also stops working when it expires; the card shows the date.
- The record keeps the calls made with a revoked key, under its id.
