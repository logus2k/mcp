# What a skill is, and what belongs here

A **skill** is an instruction a model should follow, owned here, versioned, and offered to the apps that need it. An
MCP client receives it as a **prompt** (`prompts/get`, with its arguments filled in) and as a **resource**
(`skill://name`, the text as written).

## What belongs here

Instruction that **several** consumers must apply the **same way**, and that someone must be able to change in one
place:

- the citation convention for knowledge-base passages (`cite_with_tag`);
- the European Portuguese rule (`pt_pt_style`);
- the disclosure that an answer was generated by a model (`ai_generated_disclosure`).

## What does not belong here

An application's own **system prompt**, tuned to its model and measured in its own tests. Cortex's and Slides' prompts
stay in their repositories.

## The parts of a skill

- **Content** — the instruction, in Markdown. `{{name}}` marks an argument.
- **Arguments** — what a client may fill in (`kb_tool`, for example), each with a description, whether it is
  required, and a default.
- **Apps** — who is offered it. **Expects tools** — the tools the instruction refers to; the console warns when an
  app is not offered them here (they may be the app's own). **Tags** — to find it.
