# Edit a skill and its arguments

## Steps

1. Open the skill and click **Edit**.
2. Change the content, the arguments, the apps, the tags or the tools.
3. Click **Save…**: the diff is shown; click **Save skill**.

## Notes

- The name cannot change.
- Every save is a new version; **History** compares and restores them as for a tool.
- Renaming an argument: change it in the arguments list and in the content.
