# Edit a tool

Every change is shown as a **diff** before it is saved, and saved as a new **version** you can come back to.

## Steps

1. Open the tool and click **Edit** (the tab, or the button on the Overview).
2. Change what you need: the description, the apps, the tier, the schema, the config.
3. Click **Save…**: the lines that change are shown, removed ones in red, added ones in green.
4. Click **Save tool** to apply, or **Cancel** to go back to the form.

## Notes

- The **name** cannot change: create a new tool and delete the old one instead.
- The **implementation** must exist in this version of the service; the list offers the shipped ones. A tool whose
  implementation is missing is flagged as an **orphan** in Settings and on the Overview.
- Who saved, and when, is in **History**.
