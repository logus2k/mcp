# Enable, disable or delete a skill

A disabled skill disappears from every app's prompts and resources; its definition and history are kept. Deleting
removes it from every app; its history is kept.

## Steps

- **Enable or disable**: the switch at the end of the skill's row, or on its Overview.
- **Delete**: open the skill, click **Delete** on its Overview, read the confirmation, click **Delete skill**.
