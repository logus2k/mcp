# Enable or disable a tool

A disabled tool disappears from every app's tool list and refuses calls, but keeps its definition, its history and its
record. Use it to withdraw a tool for a while without deleting it.

## Steps

1. In **Tools**, click the switch at the end of the tool's row; or open the tool and click the switch on its
   **Overview**.
2. The change is saved at once and announced at the bottom of the page.

## Notes

- A call to a disabled tool is refused as *tool disabled* and recorded as an error.
- Disabling a tool offered to several apps withdraws it from all of them. To withdraw it from one app only, edit its
  apps.
