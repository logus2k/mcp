# The menus

The top menu holds what you do across the console; the side menu holds the views.

## The top menu

- **Catalog** — *New tool…*, *New skill…*, *Refresh*, *Export the registry*, *Export the <app> app* (when Tools is
  filtered by an app), *Import (dry run)…*.
- **Apps** — one entry per app, with its tools and keys; it opens the app's card. *Show every app in Tools* clears
  the filter.
- **Activity** — the window (*Last hour* to *Last 30 days*), *Only errors*, *Export CSV*.
- **View** — the views, *Collapse the menu* / *Expand the menu*, *Dark theme*.
- **Monitoring** — the service and each backend with its state, the keys mode, how many items need attention,
  *Refresh health*.
- **Help** — *User Manual*, *Integration Guide*, *Technical Architecture*, *Keyboard Shortcuts…*, *Connect an app
  or an agent…*, the MCP endpoint's address, *About*.

A ticked entry (red, bold) is the current choice; a note in brackets after an entry is a fact, not a choice.

## The side menu

**Overview**; **Tools**, **Skills**, **Apps**; **Activity**; **Settings** at the bottom. The round button on the
menu's edge collapses it to icons and expands it again; the choice is remembered. On a tablet the menu starts as the
icon rail and the button opens it over the page. On a phone the views are the bar at the bottom, and the menus are
behind the ☰ button at the top left.
