# Delete a tool

Deleting removes the tool from every app it is offered to. Its history and its calls are kept.

## Steps

1. Open the tool and click **Delete** on its **Overview**.
2. Read the confirmation: it says how many apps lose the tool.
3. Click **Delete tool**, or **Keep**.

## Notes

- To withdraw a tool from one app only, edit its apps instead.
- To stop a tool temporarily, disable it instead.
